    Road to Platinum

    Five phases, ordered from the official trophy descriptions. Story first (with missable warnings in mission order), then strands and the finale, then the open-world sweep, then crime, combat and money, and finally cleanup to 100%. Estimated total: 60–100 hours, dominated by 100% completion and the big counters (250 stunts, 500 choices, $250,000 laundered). No difficulty trophy exists in the official list, and nothing in the list requires online play — every trophy is a single-player activity.

    ⚠ Point of no return: before starting the final mission, clear every open stranger strand and attempt the VCIA departure trophy. The finale is the last guaranteed story window.

    Missables — do not leave these behind

    Every trophy this guide flags as missable, with the window in which it can be earned and how it can be lost. “Missable” here is reasoned from the official descriptions: stranger strands that depend on a living, available giver; a unique one-off hunt; scripted encounters; and counters fed by finite choices. Everything not listed here can be cleaned up in free roam after the story.
